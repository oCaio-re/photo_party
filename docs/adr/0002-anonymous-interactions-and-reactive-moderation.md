# Anonymous Interactions and Reactive Moderation for Photos

Guests interact with photos (likes and comments) using client-side anonymous session identifiers stored in `localStorage` rather than requiring user accounts, prioritizing zero-friction participation during live events. Comments are published immediately to foster live engagement, paired with reactive moderation allowing hosts to remove any comment and guests to delete their own. Likes and comments remain open after the upload window closes, enabling guests to continue engaging with the event memories post-event.
