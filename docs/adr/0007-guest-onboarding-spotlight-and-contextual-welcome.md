# Guest Onboarding Spotlight and Contextual Table Welcome

The guest experience prioritizes friction-free, multi-generational usability for wedding attendees of all ages arriving via Table QR codes. Instead of intrusive full-screen popups or dense option matrices, the interface implements a dual-layer onboarding pattern: a 3-step lightweight Spotlight Tour on initial arrival (highlighting photo capture, the live gallery, and wedding moments, with immediate skip and persistent completion state in local storage) paired with a persistent in-page Guest Welcome Card acknowledging the table and summarizing the 3-step loop (snap photo, sign dedication, watch on live slideshow).

The upload flow replaces technical jargon ("Upload") with clear Portuguese actions ("Tirar Foto" / "Enviar para o Telão"), auto-selects the active Wedding Moment according to celebration time, applies vintage analog styling by default, and groups secondary options unobtrusively. Upon photo submission, guests receive celebratory visual confirmation with two distinct next-step pathways ("Ver Minha Foto na Galeria" or "Tirar Mais Uma Foto"). In the Live Gallery, over 30 horizontal tabs are consolidated into 3 prominent filter pills (all photos, current table, and top liked), and the expanded photo modal provides an explicit "Salvar no Celular" button to ensure non-technical and elderly guests can easily download memories.
